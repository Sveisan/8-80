## What the service is

Once a week, at a time you choose, we ring you. An AI mentor asks what you
committed to last week, what happened, and what the one thing for next week is.
Afterwards you get an email with the one thing you named.

The voice on the call is an AI. It is not a person, it is not a therapist, and
it is not a substitute for medical, psychological, legal or financial advice.
If you are in crisis, please contact your doctor, the emergency services, or
Mental Helse on 116 123.

## What we need from you

A phone number you control, an email address, and a time. You must be at least
18. You must not sign somebody else up — we send a code to the number before
any call is scheduled, and entering a number that is not yours is a misuse of
the service and of the person who owns it.

After the pilot call, you may choose to subscribe at NOK 189 for the first paid
month and NOK 249 per month from the first renewal. Payment is optional and

## Separate Beliefs programme

Beliefs is an optional AI self-reflection practice for adults, separate from the
weekly service and its subscription. One payment, equal to one monthly payment
for weekly 8&80 in the same currency, covers one chosen outcome and related

Start with one or two genuine prepared decisions; no compulsory backlog is

from the private account. Opening your history does not book a call or start
another programme. A further full programme, including revisiting the same

Where connection evidence is missing, contact support; missing evidence is not
used to shorten the guarantee. A request is acknowledged separately from a
provider-confirmed refund. Requests pause that programme's calls for human review.

Completed history follows the Beliefs retention choices described in Privacy.

## Stopping weekly calls

You can stop the calls at any time, immediately, without asking anybody:

- reply STOP to any text from us,
- press "Pause calls" under Plan and billing on the page linked in every text we send,
- or email {{support_email}}.

Stopping ends the calls. It does not by itself cancel a subscription or delete
your data. The call controls offer separate renewal cancellation and deletion
actions after phone verification. If billing cannot be reached, cancellation is
reported as unconfirmed and subscribed accounts are retained until it is resolved.

## When we might call and when we might not

We aim to ring at the time you chose. Phone networks fail, our suppliers fail,
and occasionally we will break something ourselves. A missed call is not a
breach of these terms; if we miss one, we text you a link to move it.

We do not leave voicemails.
