# legal/

Drafts. Not advice, and not reviewed by anybody qualified.

These are written to be accurate about what the software actually does — every
claim in `privacy.md` about what is stored, encrypted, kept or deleted is
checked against the code, and there is a test that fails if a claim and the
schema disagree. That is the half worth having from here.

The other half is somebody who knows Norwegian consumer and data protection law
reading them before the first person pays. In particular: the fourteen-day
right of withdrawal for digital services, what has to be in the terms for a
recurring subscription sold to a consumer, and whether the company needs to
name a data protection officer.

`{{company}}`, `{{orgnr}}` and `{{address}}` are filled in from environment
variables at render time. Until they are set, the pages say so rather than
inventing a company.
