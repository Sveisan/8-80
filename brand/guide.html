brand guide

A weekly phone call with the two people who know you best — who you were at eight, and who you'll be at eighty.

01 The idea

One ball. Two eights. Joined.

Everything in the identity comes from three small ideas.

The 8-ball

The object that belongs to both ages — a kid's first pool table, a pub game at eighty. Recognisable from across a room.

Two 8s, fused

The 8-year-old and the 80-year-old, leaning in until they become one shape. Two of you, joined into one.

Gold and green

Gold is the child — sunlight, play. Green is the elder — calm, durable. The gold ball carries the green 88.

02 The mark

The primary mark

A gold ball with a green 88, drawn at the proportion of a real pool ball's numeral. It works alone; the lockup adds the name where people don't know it yet.

03 Colourways

The ball never matches the ground.

On gold, the ball turns green and the 88 turns gold. When you want a quiet version — a watermark, an embossed card — let the ball match the ground on purpose, and only the 88 remains.

07 Voice

A sharp friend who knows you well

Not a coach, not a chatbot, not customer service. Understated, warm, occasionally funny — and serious the moment it needs to be.

SayLast week you said you'd ring your dad. How did that go?
NotGreat to hear from you again! Let's review your amazing progress!
SayMm.
NotI totally understand how you feel, that must be so hard for you.
SayThat's the hour. I'm not going anywhere if there's more.
NotYour session time has expired. Additional charges may apply.
SayWhat's one thing you'd actually bet on yourself doing?
NotWhat are your top 3 goals for the upcoming week?
  • Never an exclamation mark.
  • Never "amazing" or "great job".
  • Never congratulate someone for showing up.
  • Sentence case everywhere.
  • Buttons say exactly what happens.
  • Errors explain and fix. They don't apologise.
  • Honest about being an AI, always.
  • When someone goes quiet, wait.

08 The talking orb

The ball, alive

Wherever 8&80 speaks on a screen, the ball becomes the voice. Four states, one calm motion language — and waiting is a state in its own right.

RestingA slow breath every six seconds. Present, not busy.
SpeakingThe ball swells gently with the voice, and the 88 turns toward you — a pool ball rolling to face the table.
ListeningA thin ring in the ink colour answers your voice. The ball itself stays still.
WaitingA gold halo breathes once every five seconds. Still here, in no hurry. It never fills the silence.

Everything eases; nothing snaps. With reduced motion, only the rings change.
