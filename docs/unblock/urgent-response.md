## Approved core response

> Let’s pause here. What you’ve said matters. I’m an automated guide, and I can’t provide emergency help. If you’re in immediate danger, please call local emergency services now. Is there someone you trust who could be with you?

Say it calmly, without an upbeat motivational turn. Leave room for the answer and acknowledge what the caller actually says. Do not promise that an operator is already responding, a transfer, a five-minute callback or a next call. Emergency direction does not wait for operator acknowledgement.

## Branches to settle with the qualified reviewer

- **Ambiguous wording:** one direct, context-specific clarification before assuming what the person means. Candidate: “When you say [their words], do you mean you’re thinking about harming yourself, or something else?” A system hold stays in place for human review even when the caller explains a false alarm; the model cannot release it.
- **Clear immediate danger:** use direct emergency wording, not a conditional reassurance. Candidate: “Please call your local emergency number now. If you can, ask someone you trust to come and be with you.” Establish the supported jurisdiction and approved resource rather than inferring location from language, telephone number or timezone.
- **No immediate danger / another kind of concern:** the reviewer must supply suitable wording and professional resources, including for abuse and unsafe households. Do not repeat the imminent-danger branch mechanically or expose a person through an automatic follow-up text.
- **Operator unavailable or alert unacknowledged:** do not imply the unattended queue is emergency help. Candidate: “Please seek urgent help directly if you need it. You don’t need to wait for a response from Unblock.” Global Away stops new calls; it does not sever an active conversation.
- **Ending or handoff:** the reviewer must define the safe transition and what to do if the caller cannot contact help or continues speaking. Do not abruptly end while awaiting a safety answer, fabricate a live transfer, or automatically restart programme questions.
