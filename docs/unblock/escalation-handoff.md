# Escalation, disclosure and safe handoff · owner review draft

19 November 2026. **Proposed procedure, not owner-approved or published to agents.** The core spoken paragraph, Norway-only destinations and internal five-minute acknowledgement target are already approved. This is the next single owner decision. Qualified clinical and Norwegian privacy/legal review remains required before activation; owner approval of this draft does not establish a legal basis or clinical adequacy.

## Proposed sequence

1. **Pause and listen.** Keep programme saves and future calls held. Give the approved core response once, wait and acknowledge the answer. Ask one short clarification if the meaning is unclear; keyword detection alone cannot establish medical urgency. Respect a request to stop or hang up; never trap someone in the call.
2. **Direct urgent help immediately.** Use current-country confirmation and the approved destinations. For immediate medical danger in Norway, direct the caller to 113; for urgent medical help that cannot wait for an unavailable GP, without an immediate emergency, 116 117 is the destination. Outside Norway or when unknown, use local emergency services without guessing a number. This direction runs alongside the private alert and never waits for the five-minute acknowledgement target.
3. **Human escalation.** Eirik acknowledges the private incident within five minutes. If he believes someone faces acute danger, he promptly contacts the appropriate emergency service and describes the concern, rather than waiting for the target or treating the app as a dispatcher. Check the person's current location and callback details as needed; an account or phone prefix is not proof. Unknown location or inability to reach the caller must be explained to responders, never filled in by guesswork. No automatic emergency call or live transfer is added.
4. **Minimum necessary disclosure.** Explain what needs to be shared and seek permission where feasible, without delaying an acute emergency. A qualified Norwegian reviewer must specify the applicable legal basis, sensitive-data condition and exception handling, including refusal or inability to consent. Do not treat the signup checkbox as blanket emergency-disclosure permission. Share only what responders need: the immediate concern, confirmed location if known, and necessary contact details. No automatic transcript, entire account history, employer/family contact or authority notification. Record only a minimal private action/outcome audit, not a copied transcript.
5. **Honest handoff.** Never describe a queued, provider-accepted or delivered alert as a human response. Confirm a handoff only when the caller reports reaching help or a human has actually accepted responsibility, recording which basis supports that statement. Unblock cannot observe an external connection. If the caller can call directly, make room for them to do so; do not keep them on an app call that prevents contacting help. A trusted person must be chosen by the caller; do not assume family/household members are safe, especially when abuse is mentioned.
6. **No help reached / operator unavailable.** Be explicit that Unblock cannot provide emergency help and that urgent help should be sought directly. Do not promise a callback, transfer, dispatch or monitoring. If an alert fails or is not acknowledged, force Away and keep the incident/caller held. Do not abruptly end while the caller is answering a safety question. Respect a request to end. The qualified reviewer must settle exact wording and the remaining branches for inability/refusal to contact help, abuse, unknown location and a caller who continues talking; these are required launch blockers, not permission for an indefinite automated safety conversation.
7. **Afterwards.** Human resolution is separate from acknowledgement and from a confirmed external handoff. Keep future programme calls held until reviewed; resolving an incident does not cancel STOP, carrier opt-out, payment holds or automatically resume a person's programme. No automatic follow-up text that could expose someone in an unsafe household. A fresh daily availability check is required before new calls after the global block is cleared.

## Review basis

[Helsenorge: helping someone at risk](https://www.helsenorge.no/en/psykisk-helse/suicidal-thoughts-and-suicide/how-to-help-others/) supports listening and prompt emergency contact for acute danger. [Helsenorge: out-of-hours medical service](https://www.helsenorge.no/en/hjelpetilbud-i-kommunene/out-of-hours-medical-service?redirect=false) distinguishes 113 from urgent help through 116 117. These sources do not validate this automated service or its legal basis.

Approve the operating approach as the owner, then obtain the qualified sign-off and settle its exact exception wording before live enrolment. Do not enable safety/live flags or implement automated disclosures to make a test pass.
