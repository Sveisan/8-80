## Approved launch policy

1. **Initial withdrawal / full refund:** a consumer may withdraw within 14 days after entering the Unblock subscription contract, without giving a reason. For launch, voluntarily refund the whole initial NOK 249 payment even if opening, regular or light calls have taken place; no call deduction, administration fee or compulsory credit. This company policy must not shorten any statutory period extended by missing information or otherwise reduce statutory rights. It does not create a fresh statutory withdrawal period on every ordinary renewal.
2. **Early calls:** an optional, unticked request asks explicitly to start calls before the withdrawal period ends. If declined, do not start until it ends. Do not describe that request, a payment, a booked appointment or the first call as a withdrawal waiver. Because this is an ongoing subscription, do not represent one call as full performance of the contract. The voluntary full-refund rule above still applies.
3. **Renewal / pause:** cancellation stops future renewals once confirmed by the payment provider; ordinary cancellation keeps access until the paid period ends. Pausing calls continues billing, as already approved. Outside withdrawal, ordinary unused time or a missed call does not automatically create a pro-rata refund, without limiting rights for faulty or missing service.
4. **Our service failure:** where Unblock cannot provide paid service through its own fault, offer a proportionate monetary refund for the affected service; if the paid month cannot be provided, refund that month in full. Do not force an account credit. Review duplicate/incorrect charges and other complaints individually without imposing a refund-request deadline that cuts off statutory claims.
5. **Easy request and repayment:** provide a distinct “Withdraw and request a refund” account action, alongside the existing renewal-cancellation control, and accept a clear email to hei@8and80.me; the standard withdrawal form is available but never compulsory. A withdrawal request promptly stops new Unblock calls and initiates stopping renewal, independently of the refund-provider response. Confirm receipt separately from completed cancellation/refund. Return a withdrawal payment without undue delay and no later than 14 days after receiving the notice, to the original payment method unless the consumer expressly agrees otherwise. A provider failure remains an unresolved operator task; never label a mere request as money refunded.

## Primary sources checked 21 November 2026

- [Angrerettloven, chapter 6, §§ 20–26](https://lovdata.no/nav/lov/2014-06-20-27/kap6): withdrawal notice, time limit, repayment and payment for early service.
- [Angrerettloven, chapter 5](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2014-06-20-27/KAPITTEL_5): pre-contract and durable confirmation requirements, and an explicit early-service request.
- [Government withdrawal guidance](https://www.regjeringen.no/contentassets/6d6b5195d1ee4133977bf5339d1623d9/veileder-angrerett-2017.pdf): subscription services and calculation of the statutory period. This guide is dated 2017; the current statute controls later amendments, including electronic withdrawal requirements.

The approved full initial refund even after calls is a voluntary business policy, not a claim that every used service is legally refundable in full. It preserves more favourable mandatory rights and requires qualified review of Unblock's exact service classification and checkout materials before launch.

## Seven-day question

The owner asked whether the 14 days could be seven. Angrerettloven §§ 3 and 21 do not allow a seven-day replacement for this mandatory consumer withdrawal period. Keep the approved 14-day full-initial-refund launch policy. The extra promise of a full refund even after calls is a business choice; it is not described as mandatory in every used-service case. Any change to that voluntary promise needs a revised owner decision without reducing statutory rights.

## Operational controls and remaining approval

- `consumer-policy.ts` calculates the Oslo deadline, including weekends/public holidays and missing/late information. Actual provider contract and payment dates stay distinct from callback arrival. Canonical contract proof is tied to its subscription; older/unverified records remain held without fabricated consent.
- Pre-payment review requires matching contract email entries and terms-version acceptance. Early start is separate and initially unticked. The encrypted information snapshot and email address are separate from weekly-call memory/contact preferences.
- The account offers a fresh-Unblock-authenticated withdrawal action and downloadable acknowledgement, available under STOP/review/pause. Caller-lock transactions deduplicate requests without moving deadlines. New call eligibility is blocked independently of billing/provider state. Cancellation retries and repayment proof cannot undo that block.
- The scheduler reconciles delivered contractual mail, holds calls until the appropriate start, and retries withdrawal cancellation separately from refunds. Failed evidence reads preserve authoritative paid standing. Weekly exports exclude Unblock legal-message payloads and refund metadata. Account deletion unlinks minimal unresolved repayment records and cannot resurrect the account on a late checkout callback.
- Refunds themselves are performed manually in the provider console. `npm run unblock:refunds -- --list` is private; never copy its rows into local reports. `--review REQUEST_ID INVOICE_ID AMOUNT_MINOR --reviewed` records a proportionate amount for a non-guaranteed case. `--confirm REQUEST_ID REFUND_ID --provider-reviewed` performs read-only canonical verification; pending, partial or cross-payment evidence cannot claim a full initial refund. `--resolve-review REQUEST_ID --reviewed-no-refund` records a completed human review without claiming repayment and cannot waive a guaranteed refund.
- For a withdrawal received by email, use `--withdraw ENROLLMENT_ID --received-at ISO_TIMESTAMP --customer-notice` with the actual inbox receipt time (not the processing time or an untrusted sender Date header). An earlier notice can shorten an unresolved deadline; later retries never extend it. A resolved/refunded case needs separate human review rather than rewriting its repayment evidence. Check the private request list each workday and process approaching deadlines before weekends/Away. Inspect contractual notice failures in the existing private message tools. Missing provider permissions or delivery proof must be resolved before calls can begin; do not label queue/acceptance as delivery or a request as refunded.
- The exact-version `UNBLOCK_CONSUMER_NOTICES_APPROVED` gate stays off until the sender, reply address, exact templates and automatic recipient rules in [consumer notice drafts](consumer-notices.md) have owner approval. Changing their text or routing requires a new version/approval. This implements the root AGENTS.md final-send requirement; no email or SMS was sent for this change.
- Stripe merchant/tax/itemised-receipt verification, qualified consumer/privacy/clinical review and explicit audience/activation approval remain separate. Real call/alert tests are owner-waived and remain unverified.
