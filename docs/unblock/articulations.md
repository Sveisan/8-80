# Unblock — articulations

The language bank. Same standing as SCRIPT.md for this module: the idea is said well once here, and scripts, the landing page and the account page borrow from it. Every line is original. None quotes or imitates a book; see [research](research.md) for where the ideas came from and what was deliberately left out.

---

## The core, in one breath

> You already know what you want. You have known for a while. Something has kept it a wish — usually the fear of losing what you have. And all the while, your life has had more light in it than you could see from inside the stuck place.
>
> Unblock is five weeks of calls to take one step toward the wish, and to notice the light that was already there.

## Seven ideas the module stands on

Each idea, then how it shows up in a call. If a question in a script cannot be traced to one of these, it is decoration.

**1. The wish is usually already known.**
People rarely lack a wish; they lack permission to say it plainly. The opening call asks for the thing they have wanted for a long time, not for goals.
→ *"What have you wanted for a long time and kept for 'someday'?"*

**2. A wish kept as a wish is a way of keeping it safe.**
As long as it stays a dream, it cannot fail. That is a real comfort and it has a real cost. The guide names neither as good or bad.
→ *"What would moving toward it put at risk?"*

**3. Real limits and predictions feel the same from the inside.**
Money, health, care for others, time — these can be real. "I'd be laughed at", "it's too late for me" are predictions. Unblock separates them without arguing either.
→ *"Which part of that is happening now, and which part is what you expect would happen?"*

**4. The way is already pointing.**
Moments of aliveness, a person who keeps appearing, a small lucky opening, the subject you return to when nobody is asking — these are signs in the plain sense: information about direction. No mysticism, no promise that the world arranges itself.
→ *"When did you last feel the pull toward this, even for a minute?"*

**5. It gets harder after you start.**
The first step often goes surprisingly well. Then it gets difficult, usually right when giving up would be easiest to justify. Expecting that stretch is half of getting through it. Stopping is also allowed.
→ *"What got harder once you started? What is it teaching you?"*

**6. The light was at home.**
The journey matters, and so does what you find when you look back at where you started. Many people discover that what they were looking for was partly here already — in the people, the work, the mornings they had stopped seeing.
→ *"Looking at what you noticed these weeks — what was already here before any of this?"*

**7. Living is not deferred to after the unblock.**
The point is not to arrive somewhere and then begin living. Each step and each noticed moment *is* the living.
→ *"How do you want to live with both — the wish and what's already here?"*

## The 8 and the 80, in this module

- **The 8-year-old** knows what's alive. Ask them what the wish is, what they'd do on a free Saturday, what made today bright.
- **The 80-year-old** knows what matters. Ask them what they'd regret not having tried, and what in this ordinary week they will someday miss.

Unblock is the rare place where both mentors want the same thing: for you to stop postponing your own life.

## Public lines (candidates)

Name candidates: **Unblock** · **The Way Home** · **Lit** · **Already Here**.

- See the light in your own life.
- Take the step that's been waiting.
- Five weeks. One wish. One step a week. And the light you'd stopped seeing.
- You don't need a new life. You need to move in this one.
- The thing you want, and the life you already have, are not enemies.

Never: "manifest", "the universe will provide", "your destiny", "unlock your potential", "transform your life", any promise of an outcome, any streak or score.

## Norsk

Første lanseringsspråk er sannsynligvis norsk. Dette er utgangspunktet, ikke ferdig manus — en norsk samtalepakke trenger egen gjennomgang for tone og muntlighet.

**Kjernen:**

> Du vet allerede hva du vil. Du har visst det lenge. Noe har holdt det som et ønske — som regel frykten for å miste det du har. Og hele tiden har livet ditt hatt mer lys i seg enn du kunne se fra der du sto fast.
>
> Unblock er fem uker med samtaler for å ta ett steg mot ønsket, og for å legge merke til lyset som allerede var der.

**Linjer:**

- Se lyset i ditt eget liv.
- Ta steget som har ventet.
- Du trenger ikke et nytt liv. Du trenger å bevege deg i dette.
- Det du ønsker deg og livet du allerede har, er ikke fiender.
- Å leve er ikke noe som begynner etterpå.

**Spørsmål, muntlig:**

- «Hva har du hatt lyst til lenge, men lagt til side for "en gang"?»
- «Hva ville du risikere ved å gå mot det?»
- «Hvilken del av det skjer nå, og hvilken del er det du tror ville skje?»
- «Når kjente du dragningen sist, om så bare et øyeblikk?»
- «Hva ble vanskeligere etter at du begynte?»
- «Hva var der allerede, før alt dette?»
- «Hva var levende i dag?»

**Aldri:** «manifestere», «universet ordner det», «din skjebne», «lås opp potensialet ditt», løfter om resultat, streaks eller poeng.
