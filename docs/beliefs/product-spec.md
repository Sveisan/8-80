## Purpose

**Short daily calls to practise decisions grounded in your own experience.**

Help an adult examine an assumption that obstructs a desired change, choose an honest decision in their own words, recall supporting experience, acknowledge real difficulty and find an opportunity to act. Repetition and lived experience are proposed mechanisms. Do not promise treatment, reprogramming, certain outcomes or that a practical constraint is faulty thinking.

Weekly 8&80 provides perspective, commitments and accountability. Beliefs is independently enrolled and purchased. Share brand, verified identity, encrypted infrastructure, support, global STOP and human safety holds. Keep consent, entitlement, schedules, outcome, belief backlog, decisions, evidence, scores and completion separate. Module calls never advance the weekly call counter, change its singular memories, extend its trial or alter its subscription. There is no implemented personal-context-sharing choice; therefore no automatic transfer is allowed.

The existing private Beliefs account is sufficient; no new account hierarchy is introduced. `/start` is a neutral route to weekly 8&80. A module-only customer does not need a weekly slot or weekly onboarding. No sales interruption belongs in a difficult reflection.

## Preparation and daily practice

Sequence: desired change → concrete situation → real obstacle → caller-recognised belief → examine experience → caller-authored decision → genuine supporting experience → balanced reflection → opportunity → repeat/review.

A prepared belief has the caller's confirmed old thought and trigger, replacement decision, at least one real experience, remaining difficulty/uncertainty and a relevant opportunity. Missing content stays missing. The caller may disagree, pass, stop or revise their focus. Do not require an opposite statement about time, resources, health, access or support. Evidence gives a reason to trust a plausible decision, not a guarantee.

Queued items wait until a practice slot is available. When selected, check fit and freshly confirm preparation before activation; even legacy stored preparation must not activate silently. The account offers preparation only for an available selected slot, while active decisions remain editable.

| Call | Experience | Durable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding / continuation | Recognisable availability greeting; purpose/duration/rhythm before reflection; choose outcome; map/order genuine beliefs; fully prepare one or two; explain use and verified next booking | Signed caller-word reconciliation saves complete or genuine partial stages. Understanding starts daily/weekly scheduling only when the outcome and at least one fully prepared decision exist; no backlog is required |
| Daily | One scheduled call, flat frequency; initially two decisions, cap three; identify topic then decision, real experience (older allowed), balanced reflection; may pass | Complete exercise coverage is distinct from passed/incomplete coverage. No discovery, preparation, score, sales or catch-up ringing. No prepared active items means no daily placement |
| Weekly | Separate review; improvement, no change or poor fit are equally valid; reflect on each item active at start; caller chooses/explicitly confirms 0–10 score; prepare next ordered backlog item if it still fits | Server-owned period, one score per item/period. Two consecutive periods at 0–1 retire. Missing period, higher score or changed decision breaks sequence. Newly prepared/revised items cannot score in this call |
| Around day 28 | Natural usefulness, concrete change, burden and rhythm questions in a weekly review | Server `first_month_review` context identifies period 3. Confirmed answers can enter ordinary reflections; there is no separate automated benefit score or additional scheduled call |

Targets are 25–30 minutes onboarding, 60–90 seconds daily and 10–15 minutes weekly. These targets do not prove feasibility and never justify rushing speech, silence or required content. Provider audio review remains necessary.

Retirement removes daily practice, not a diagnosis of cure. Previous decision versions and their evidence/reviews are archived privately when wording changes or an unfinished programme reactivates an item. This release cannot reconstruct historical words already overwritten by older code. Scores reset on changed decisions. Reactivation stays within cap and is unavailable after programme completion.

## First-month account experience

Join uses understandable name wording, editable `+47`, other international codes, explicit adult/AI/service consent and phone verification. Optional contact email has an account-support purpose; existing shared identity details are preserved. Verification expires after the configured ten minutes; private access expires after one hour and requires fresh phone proof.

For a new programme, the verified signup's chosen name and optional support email are retained inside its encrypted practice record. A combined customer can choose a Beliefs name/contact without overwriting weekly 8&80 details. The voice agent receives only the chosen name (shared verified name as fallback), never the support address. Rejoining an existing enrollment preserves that programme and its existing preferences; returning access does not recollect them. Corrections to contact preferences currently go through support rather than an invented profile editor.

Choose an explicit IANA scheduling zone with Oslo fallback, first conversation, daily time and separate weekly day/time. Next appointment displays in the browser's local zone when available; fallback is saved zone. This is display-only. Changing usual times shows saved scheduling zone and does not require another onboarding date after onboarding. Moving/skipping one appointment leaves usual times intact. A moved recurring occurrence retains its following regular appointment and cannot be moved past it. Repeat submissions with stale appointment timestamps are rejected. All controls respect payment, STOP, safety, pause, coverage and collisions; a call being placed cannot be moved.

Account labels consistently describe **outcome**, **decisions being practised**, **supporting experiences**, **remaining difficulty and opportunity**, **queued beliefs** and **completed decisions/history**. Editing a decision explains its effect on future calls and the score sequence. Next call and its controls are prominent. Back/home/sign-out remain available; private history is never in an external message or bearer URL. See [actual route/recovery map](first-month-map.md).

## Integrity, privacy and safety

Voice commands are staged in an encrypted session snapshot. Responses are provisional. Only verified signed completion containing actual consecutive caller turns, including the final explicit agreement where required, changes canonical practice. Agent read-back is not caller evidence. Missing words, final disagreement, expired sessions, altered retries, holds or concurrent edits prevent a claimed save. Short confirmations need no artificial long recital; full actual caller turns must remain intact.

Personal aggregates, histories, evidence, coverage and session snapshots are encrypted at rest. Private routes use scoped expiring cookies, CSRF, no-store/no-referrer, escaped text and a narrowly hashed display script. Base links cannot open Beliefs history. Complete owner export includes both products; older weekly exports exclude Beliefs and require fresh proof. Whole-account deletion stops both products and cascades local records; provider erasure/retention must be verified separately.

Keyword detection is a deterministic signal with false positives and misses. Negated, historic or third-party mentions may hold a call. Indirect/transcription wording can be missed. Scores and inferred tone are not risk assessments. During-call checks and post-call checks share account holds; global STOP outranks module resume. Tier-1 notices and tier-2 digest intent use the durable outbox. Provider acceptance is not delivery, acknowledgment or staffed human response. A support inbox is not emergency coverage.

## Pilot evidence and completion

Before live participants, agree target and review owners for: caller understanding; decision/evidence/balance and score coverage; day-7/day-28 return; usefulness and burden; concrete reported action; duration/latency; passes/withdrawal; completed programmes versus unwanted abandonment; total connected minutes/provider costs; operator workload and response failures. No numerical success targets are invented. A falling score alone does not demonstrate effectiveness.

Local software completion means fresh type/lint/disposable-DB and journey checks plus reviewable contracts/docs. Live readiness additionally requires qualified urgent protocol/coverage, script approval, real attached tools/variables/signed callbacks/AMD/audio, one-time payment delivery/refunds/returns, approved legal/retention information and a controlled pilot. All release gates stay off until their actual external prerequisites and separate activation authorization are satisfied.
