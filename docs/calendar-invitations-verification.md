# Calendar invitation verification

## Live delivery test — 13 November 2026

Three one-off test messages were sent through the connected Resend account to the
owner's existing 8&80 recap address. All three were independently retrieved from
the provider and reported **delivered**. Each used the product's established
sender and a `.ics` attachment generated by the calendar serializer in this PR.

| Case | Test appointment, Europe/Oslo | Calendar method | Sequence | Event status | Provider delivery |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Invitation | 17 November, 08:00–08:15 | `REQUEST` | 0 | `CONFIRMED` | Delivered |
| Move | 17 November, 09:00–09:15 | `REQUEST` | 1 | `CONFIRMED` | Delivered |
| Cancellation | Latest 09:00–09:15 appointment | `CANCEL` | 2 | `CANCELLED` | Delivered |

The sent attachments were compared directly. All three preserve the same `UID`,
organizer, attendee and event title. The move changes only the appointment time
and revision metadata; the cancellation targets the moved appointment's exact
start and end. Each MIME `method` agrees with the calendar's `METHOD`, and each
update has a later `DTSTAMP` and a higher `SEQUENCE`. Separate persisted email
idempotency keys prevent retrying one test from generating an extra message.

`TEST`. No real phone call was scheduled, no caller profile was changed and no
production deployment was made.

## Calendar client result

**Awaiting the owner's result.** Confirm that the invitation creates one event,
the time change updates that same event without duplication, and cancellation
removes or marks that event cancelled in the receiving calendar application.

Provider delivery and attachment identity checks establish successful delivery
and the intended update protocol. They do not establish how Gmail, Outlook or
Apple Calendar actually rendered or applied the messages. Client compatibility
must not be reported as passed before that result is observed or reported.
