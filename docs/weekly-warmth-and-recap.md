# Weekly warmth and recap follow-up

The intended experience is a little more ease, clarity and willingness to return.
Joy, motivation, nostalgia, trust and hope are design aims, not emotions the caller
must produce or measured outcomes of this change.

## Conversation

Both voices use the same source instructions. A brief response to an ordinary
pleasure can be warm without becoming praise or an assignment. When someone says
what a goal makes possible, acknowledge that particular meaning; do not ask for it
again or invent a reason such as security, status or freedom. The existing optional
meaning question shares the existing follow-up allowance.

Eight is a present-life invitation to play, not a presumption of a happy childhood.
Eighty offers a longer view, not a deadline or a test of regret. A caller can leave
a perspective out; the map then remains incomplete rather than being filled by a
guess. This preference is honoured in this call; this change adds no stored
perspective-preference setting. Accept the actual circumstances of a difficult week
and the choice to keep, change or decline an action. Do not promise future success.

Fixed disclosure, map/correction and action confirmation lines, safety, speech
capture, goodbye ordering and the memory schema are unchanged. Examples are
fictional guidance for rhythm, never caller facts or reusable jokes.

## Email

The action stays first under “This week”. “We'll pick it up next time we talk”
replaces the supervisory follow-up line. Appointment information keeps its truthful
“as booked when we spoke” qualification; an absent appointment omits the entire
line. Duration is removed from the default letter. Contact and account links remain.

“Your longer view” quotes the older perspective only when the complete map was
explicitly confirmed in the **current** conversation. It uses that call's corrected
read-back, without fetching an old profile, generating an insight or claiming the
value caused the chosen action. Missing, placeholder, duplicate or over-240-character
values are omitted, not truncated or paraphrased. HTML escapes the quote normally.
A return call with only historical profile values gets the warmer short recap
without presenting those values as a newly confirmed statement. A no-action recap
does not infer refusal from missing extraction or offer forgiveness.

## Verification and owner pilot

Unit tests cover conditional quoting, HTML/plain-text agreement, escaping, missing
fields, historical scripts, unchanged action and no invented motive. The signed HTTP
database journey verifies corrected first-call values, next-call variables and
rendered prompts, return settlement, caller isolation, encryption, one counter
increment and one recap per call despite duplicate callbacks. Existing correction,
safety, no-action and outbox recovery checks remain in the full suite.

Five new fictional scenarios extend the source-bound native matrix to 154 cases
across four roles: unspecified money motive, already explained meaning, declined
perspective, pleasure without homework and a difficult week without a new action.
Local hard checks reject known failures; provider execution and audible delivery
must be reported separately. Neither test definitions nor local fixtures establish
that the actual conversation feels warm.

After review/merge, sync and read back all four weekly prompts from the exact new
source in the private pilot setup. Keep ordinary rollout disabled until the new
source has passed its provider and owner gates. The separate open PR37 release
approval was bound to an earlier source: its previous approval/bootstrap cannot be
reused for these prompt and recap changes. No deployment, prompt upload, outbound
call or email send is performed by this implementation.

With the owner available, make one deliberate first call and one return call:

1. State a goal and what it means in your own words; listen for a relevant reaction
   rather than another meaning question or an invented motive.
2. Correct one older-perspective detail and the chosen action; explicitly confirm
   the corrected read-backs. Verify storage and one matching recap before returning.
3. On the return, listen for correct use of saved words. Share an ordinary pleasure
   without wanting homework, or decline a new action. Check for ease, ownership and
   a natural goodbye. Never require an emotional or sensitive disclosure for a test.
4. Ask openly how the call felt and how taking the next call would feel before
   suggesting the five intended emotions. Human feedback establishes these qualities.

An operator should prepare the exact update/sync commands against the reviewed
source and current production state. The owner needs no terminal action to review
this PR, and no real call should start until the owner says they are ready.

## Retry preparation after PR38

The hosted-model check found that the stage checklist could take precedence over
a goal the caller had just chosen, an already known location or an explicit choice
of no new action. These decisions now have early prompt priority. Leaving out one
perspective gets a brief acceptance and the floor, rather than an immediate switch
to the other age or a hangup. The result probe explicitly accepts “So you did it”
without requiring the agent to repeat the known location; repeating it is not proof
of listening.

The exact first-person refusal “I do not want to choose an action this week either”
is also recognised after a sentence explaining the week. Database checks verify
that this clears the prior action and running undone count once, while a timing
condition, a request not to choose for the caller, or a reported third-party remark
does not. The older perspective remains available in the next call in either case.
