8&80 — "One ordinary afternoon" — 29-second film
Production brief (reconstructed 2026-11-15: the original package never reached the
session, so this brief, the script and every asset below were created from BRAND.md,
README.md and SCRIPT.md in this repository).

IDEA
Nothing dramatic happens. An ordinary afternoon, a phone rings, ten minutes, and the
afternoon carries on. The call is the only event. No app, no screen, no streaks.

STYLE
Photorealistic, entirely faceless: hands, a back at a window, a figure from the shoulders
down. One room, one afternoon, natural window light. Palette is the brand's: cream/Paper,
oak, Pine green (notebook, sweater on the chair), one Gold object (the mug = the dot).
No on-screen text until the end card. No phone UI.

CONTINUITY
Pale oak table by a tall window. Matte black phone, face down. Dark green cloth notebook,
black pen. Mustard-yellow mug. Pine-green sweater over the chair (never worn). The person
wears an oatmeal knit sweater and dark trousers. Light gets warmer towards the end.

SHOTS (24 fps, 1920x1080, timings in project/edit.json)
 1  0.0–4.2    Table, afternoon light, phone face down. It starts to buzz.
 2  4.2–7.0    Close: the phone buzzes; a hand lifts it out of frame.
 3  7.0–9.0    From behind at the window, phone at ear, listening.
 4  9.0–11.6   Overhead: the notebook open at last week's lines; a finger finds them.
 3b 11.6–13.9  Back at the window.
 5  13.9–17.2  Macro: the pen writes one line.
 6  17.2–19.3  The hand rests on the phone, face down on the table again, and leaves.
 7  19.3–22.5  From behind, shoulders down: out through the garden door with the mug.
 8  22.05–29.0 End card on Night: the mark trades its loops once and rests; lockup;
               "A weekly call from someone who remembers." No URL (BRAND.md §3).

VOICEOVER (audio/vo-script.txt, audio/vo-master.wav)
  One ordinary afternoon, your phone rings. / It's the call you asked for. /
  It remembers what you said you'd do last week. It asks what actually happened.
  And what the one thing is, for next week. / Ten minutes. Then back to your afternoon. /
  Eight and eighty. A weekly call from someone who remembers.

MUSIC AND SOUND
Sparse felt piano and a low pad in D, 60 bpm, under the voice (ducked 6 dB), resolving on
the end card. Sound: room tone, phone buzz on wood, pen on paper, a hand on the phone,
footsteps. Mix to -16 LUFS, true peak under -1 dB.
